Privacy Policy

Bourbon Bully – Bourbon Radar · Last updated: November 25, 2026

Bourbon Radar ("we", "us") is an app and website that helps bourbon and whiskey fans find public announcements of bottle arrivals, allocated releases, raffles, store barrel picks, festivals and tastings. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. We do not sell personal information.

Information you give us

  • Location for nearby alerts (optional). If you allow it, your device's location or the ZIP code you type is used to show distances and filter alerts. It is stored on your device. If you turn on notifications, a rounded location (about 1 km / 0.6 miles) is stored with your notification settings so we can send nearby alerts.
  • Notification settings (optional). When you turn on notifications we store your device's push address (provided by Apple, Google or your browser) together with your chosen alert types, distance, states and "verified only" choice. We use it only to send the alerts you asked for.
  • Submitted events and shared posts (optional). If you submit an event or share a post, we store what you send: event details, links, pictures you attach, and – only if you provide them – your name or store name and e-mail address. Pictures are resized and their hidden location data is removed in your browser before upload. Your e-mail address is never published; it is only used if we have a question about your submission. Approved submissions are published with the event details (not your e-mail).

Public information we collect about bourbon events and bottles

Bourbon Radar automatically reads public information published by businesses – liquor stores, distilleries, state liquor agencies and event organizers – such as event calendars, news feeds and online shop product lists, respecting each website's rules for automated access (robots.txt). With permission from Meta Platforms, we may also read public posts from business Facebook Pages and Instagram professional accounts through Meta's official API to find bottle arrivals and events. We do not read personal profiles, private groups, private messages or comments, and we do not collect information about the people who view, like or comment on posts. Each alert links back to the original post or page.

How we use information

  • To show bourbon and whiskey alerts and the distance to them.
  • To send push notifications that match your settings, and to avoid sending the same alert twice.
  • To review and publish submitted events and shared posts.
  • To keep the service working and safe (for example, limiting spam submissions).

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

Service providers

We use a small number of providers to run Bourbon Radar: Supabase (database and picture storage), GitHub (website hosting and automatic scanning), Apple, Google and Mozilla push services (delivering notifications), Expo (app notifications), the U.S. Census Bureau and OpenStreetMap (public location data) and Meta Platforms (reading public business posts, once approved). They process information only to provide their service to us.

How long we keep information

  • Notification settings: until you turn notifications off (they are deleted right away), or until the push service tells us your device no longer accepts them.
  • The list of alerts already sent to a device (used to prevent duplicates): 90 days.
  • Submissions: declined submissions and their pictures are deleted; other submissions are removed within about 60 days after review or after the event has passed.
  • Alerts based on social media posts: removed automatically a few days after the post, or after the event date.

Your choices and data deletion

Turn off notifications: open Bourbon Radar → 🔔 Alert notifications → switch notifications off. This deletes your notification settings and push address from our database immediately.

Location: you can clear or change your location in the app at any time, or deny location access in your device settings.

Delete anything else: e-mail bourbonbully@protonmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and tell us what to remove (for example, a submission you sent). Businesses can ask us to stop showing alerts from their pages the same way. We respond within 30 days.

Facebook / Instagram: Bourbon Radar does not offer Facebook or Instagram login and does not store data about Facebook or Instagram users. To ask us to remove an alert made from a business post, use the e-mail above.

Children

Bourbon Radar is about alcoholic beverages and is intended only for adults of legal drinking age (21+ in the United States). We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 21.

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections. Our database blocks public access to notification settings and submissions; only the automatic notification service and the site administrator can read them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
